Privacy Policy

Welcome to S.S.C. CLASSES. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored and protected when you use the S.S.C. CLASSES educational application.

By using the application, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

Last Updated: October 2026

1. About S.S.C. CLASSES

S.S.C. CLASSES is an educational application designed to provide students with digital learning resources, including educational videos, notes, study materials, online tests, test results, notifications and community-based learning features.

The application is intended primarily for students preparing for school-level and Bihar Board related studies.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, S.S.C. CLASSES may collect or process the following information:

Student Registration Information

  • Student name
  • Father's name
  • Village name
  • Mobile number
  • Class
  • Roll number
  • Email address, where provided
  • Profile photograph, where provided

Account Information

  • User account identifier
  • Authentication information required to sign in
  • Account creation and related account information

Learning and Test Information

  • Tests attempted by the student
  • Answers submitted during tests
  • Test scores and results
  • Learning and test-related information associated with the account

Community Chat Information

  • Messages sent through the Community Chat
  • Student display/name information associated with messages
  • Message timestamps and related technical identifiers

3. How We Use Information

Information processed by S.S.C. CLASSES may be used for purposes such as:

  • Creating and managing student accounts
  • Authenticating students and maintaining login sessions
  • Providing educational content and learning materials
  • Providing online tests and assessments
  • Calculating and displaying test results
  • Displaying student profile information
  • Providing Community Chat functionality
  • Sending relevant application notifications
  • Maintaining application functionality and security
  • Responding to support requests

4. Firebase Services

S.S.C. CLASSES uses Firebase services provided by Google to support application functionality such as authentication and cloud data storage.

Depending on the features used, Firebase may process information associated with your account and application activity.

Firebase services are used to help provide secure authentication, storage and application functionality.

For more information about Google's privacy practices, please refer to Google's official privacy documentation.

Google Privacy Policy

5. Student Data

Student information is used to provide the educational services and account-related functionality of S.S.C. CLASSES.

We do not intend to sell student personal information to third parties.

Information may be processed by service providers used to operate application functionality, including Firebase and other technical services necessary for the application.

6. Community Chat

S.S.C. CLASSES may provide a Community Chat feature that allows students to communicate within the application.

Messages submitted to the Community Chat may be stored in the application's cloud database and may be visible to other authorized users of the Community Chat.

Users should not share passwords, financial information, government identification numbers, private contact information or other sensitive information in Community Chat.

7. Profile Photograph

If a student chooses to upload a profile photograph, the photograph may be stored and displayed as part of the student's profile and related application interfaces.

Uploading a profile photograph is intended to support the profile functionality of the application.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed through the application against unauthorized access, misuse, alteration or disclosure.

However, no internet-based application or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as necessary to provide application services, maintain accounts, preserve test and result records, comply with applicable requirements, resolve disputes and maintain security.

Where appropriate, users may contact us to request account or personal-data deletion.

10. Account and Data Deletion

If you want to request deletion of your S.S.C. CLASSES account or personal information, please contact us using the support details provided below.

We may need to verify the request before processing it.

Some information may be retained where necessary for legitimate operational, security or legal purposes.

11. Children's Privacy

S.S.C. CLASSES is an educational application intended for students. Where users are minors, use of the application should be undertaken with the knowledge and appropriate supervision of a parent or legal guardian.

Parents or guardians who have questions about a student's information may contact us using the support details below.

12. Third-Party Services and Links

The application may use third-party services to provide certain functionality. These services may have their own privacy policies and terms.

S.S.C. CLASSES may also provide links to external websites or platforms. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites that are not operated by us.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the application, services, legal requirements or privacy practices.

When changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
